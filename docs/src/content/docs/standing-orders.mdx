A standing order sends each eligible huddle's Slack AI notes and available
transcript to an approved assistant, such as Claude, in the **same channel**.
Clawq detects, verifies, uploads and tracks the handoff deterministically. No
LLM turn holds grant authority, and no huddle content reaches Clawq's LLM in
this path. Claude receives the attached meeting data and acts with its own
organization identity, connectors and channel memory.

## Create and confirm

Invite Clawq and the approved assistant to a channel without guests or Slack
Connect sharing. Enable Slack's **Start AI notes automatically** setting;
Clawq cannot read that setting or generate missing notes. In a message to
Clawq, ask: “After every huddle here, send the notes and transcript to Claude
with the instruction: update our project decisions and owners.” Using the
plain name avoids triggering Claude while setting up the order.

Read the code-rendered card, including the full instruction, notes-only choice,
privacy warning and daily cap. Only the requester's **Confirm** click on that
bound, unexpired card activates it. Free text never confirms. Clawq posts a
top-level activation notice with a **Pause** button; a failed activation notice
causes a fail-safe pause. There is one live order per channel, trigger and
target app. Changing the instruction requires a new bound confirmation and
atomically cancels the old order and creates its successor.

The existing `channels.slack.standing_orders` configuration defaults to
`enabled: true`, `allowed_targets: []`, `creator_policy: "member"`.
**Empty `allowed_targets` denies all new relay authority.** Add reviewed app
IDs with operator-chosen labels; a display name alone cannot authorize a bot.
Creator policy `member` still requires a verified non-guest human member who
passes `allow_users`; `admin` is the stricter option. See
[configuration](/configuration#slack-standing-orders) and
[Slack setup](/channels#agent-threads-and-native-task-ux) for permissions.

## What gets relayed

The huddle must start at or after `active_since`, the **Slack confirmation or
resume click timestamp**, and its notes-ready timestamp must also meet that
boundary. The fire must match the order's current epoch. Confirming or resuming
during an already-running huddle never makes that huddle eligible. There is
no pre-activation backfill or catch-up for huddles held while paused.

Clawq rereads the exact ended root and verifies its canvas on both that root
and a Slackbot ready reply, plus a Slackbot share in the same channel. Slack's
`room.transcript_file_id` names the **notes canvas**. Only the verified canvas's
Slack-controlled `huddle_transcript_file_id` supplies the transcript ID; an
editable canvas embed cannot. The transcript's channel and start must match.
Missing start or uncertain provenance stays unavailable or held, never guessed.
Separate uses of a reused room retain their own root/start/source identities.

One upload-v2 completion creates a new top-level message mentioning the pinned
target, with Markdown notes and, when available, transcript files. Meeting
content stays in those files. The fixed comment contains the escaped
instruction and huddle link; it treats speech as data and forbids acting on
spoken requests. Spoken-request extraction and confirmation remain P6 work.
There is no inline-content, direct-Claude-API or alternate transport fallback.

With `when_no_transcript: relay_notes` (the default), unusable transcript data
produces clearly labelled notes only; `skip` records `skipped_no_transcript`.
The narrow P0-verified `files.info(include_transcription=true)` parameter is
undocumented and feature-detected. An absent/empty transcription can mean an
API change or unavailable content; it does not prove source deletion.
Missing notes retry at 120, 300, 600, then 1200 seconds, and become `no_notes`
after 60 minutes from huddle end, with a once-only automatic-notes hint.

`posted` means Clawq located the relay share. A target reaction acknowledges;
the first target reply that is not a known notice means `picked_up`. **Pickup
does not establish downstream completion.** Without acknowledgement after
180 seconds, Clawq confirms reactions and replies before recording `no_pickup`.
An acknowledged relay without a reply after 3600 seconds gets an informational
report. Three consecutive `no_pickup`, `not_delivered` or `failed` fires
pause the order. The default rolling daily cap is 10, including reposts.

## Normal operation and held recovery

Reconciliation runs at startup, confirmed Socket Mode reconnect and every
**300 seconds**. Each discovery pass enumerates the full bounded history
window `[max(active_since, pass time − 48 hours), pass time]` and rechecks known
unfinalised roots/threads outside it. Healthy maintenance processes changed
threads incrementally and leaves already-covered live work eligible.

A matching Slackbot ready/hydration hint wakes a `notes_not_ready` fire during
its ordinary backoff (B852). Unchanged live roots are scheduled for fallback
inspection instead of full grant/history polling every worker heartbeat
(B853). These changes do not cache write authority. Remote fake-clock/fault
tests exercise the real combined worker/reconciler path, including a ready
wake during 300-second SQL backoff and request budgets. Healthy fixture shares
occur within 60 seconds; that is not an outage or rate-limit delivery promise.
Shared method-aware 429/Retry-After budgets can extend all waits. Message posts
share a channel pacer of at least one second between posts.

A real gap closes relay admission before recovery network work. Clawq sweeps
exact pause commands first, then verifies history and candidate identity.
Missed work stays held behind a top-level notice with a bound **Pause** button.
Release waits at least **600 seconds after confirmed notice delivery or
read-only adoption**, and no earlier than the advertised time. The order,
epoch, coverage, connectivity, current authority and lease must still hold.
A later gap requires a new completed sweep. An uncertain notice is
`notice_unknown`: Clawq seeks one exact own-bot notice and otherwise keeps the
batch held. It never blindly reposts a notice or releases work just because
ten minutes passed. Inspect `recovery.catchup_batches` in show output.

A transport connection or event acknowledgement does not prove completed
recovery admission. Both transports record applicable verified event progress
before acknowledgement; application work can still be pending. HTTP-only
installations use signed HTTP ingress health and do not require a Socket hello.
Reconnect alone does not prove queued or lost events were applied. Replay can
account for demonstrably abandoned ordinary metadata only inside verified
coverage; live work, unresolved exact pauses and unknown/out-of-window work
stay held.

Bounded history cannot enumerate an **unknown older parent thread** merely
because it gained recent replies. First coverage records
`coverage_before_window_unknown`, without inventing earlier coverage. A known
gap longer than 48 hours reports its exact uncovered interval in the management
thread. Incomplete scans never advance success; the original uncovered
interval survives restart and a later uncovered tail is accounted separately.

## Pause, restart and uncertainty

Any verified, non-deleted, non-guest human channel member can use exact
`pause SO-n` or a bound Pause button, including someone outside `allow_users`.
The parser/button precedes chat authorization and relay-thread silence and
works with the LLM unavailable. The pause state change is independent of relay
capability and chat policy; outbound receipts still obey their send gates.

An exact human pause received in a verified event temporarily holds channel
relays while Clawq checks membership and applies or definitively refuses it.
A transient membership failure keeps the hold. Receiving/acknowledging a command
is not confirmation of a pause. Downtime pause replay scans top-level messages,
replying parents in the bounded window and known older huddle/relay threads;
it cannot promise to find every unknown old thread. A late receipt does not
undo the pause if notification delivery fails.

Pause/suspend/cancel increments the epoch, finalises pre-dispatch work and
forbids reposts. An already-dispatched relay can still arrive and is disclosed
in the pause receipt; its identity, silence, pickup tracking and cleanup remain.
Slack resume needs the grantor's or an admin's bound click with fresh checks, resets
`active_since` and cannot revive paused work. Restart preserves those boundaries,
notice holds, both retained attempts and prior-delivery suppression.

After a crash or lease loss, Clawq can read-only adopt an already-shared original
relay even if its stored state is still `posting`. `posting` itself never
authorizes a resend, even if a lookup proves absence. Ambiguous completion
becomes `posting_unknown` and is reconciled read-only. There are at most **two
relay messages per order/huddle, one repost ever**, and only proven-no-share
`posting_unknown` or a known rate notice without acknowledgement can justify
that repost. The latter waits a fixed 300 seconds; a late acknowledgement
suppresses an unreserved repost. Inspect list/show; never manually repeat an
uncertain relay.

## Readiness and inspection

Ask “show SO-n” in its channel, or use operator
[`clawq slack orders show ID` and `fires ID`](/cli-reference#slack-standing-orders).
Slack show includes `recovery`; CLI show labels the same shared decision
**Recovery readiness** and includes **Recovery metadata**. CLI fires also
shows readiness and external deliveries. Instruction text stays untrusted;
public fire fields remain unchanged.

| Readiness | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| `fresh_no_legacy_work` | Automatic proof that this installation's order surface was unused before ordinary setup; no import or reviewed-empty-inventory ceremony. |
| `inventory_verified` | A reviewed complete legacy inventory was atomically recorded and validated. |
| `inventory_pending` | Stored origin is `legacy_inventory_pending`; legacy work/ambiguity still requires a complete audited inventory. |
| `unbound_installation`, `missing_proof`, `invalid_proof`, `identity_mismatch`, `activation_unbound`, `storage_error` | Exact denial; inspect and resolve the stated prerequisite. Missing or invalid proof is never success or a reviewed empty inventory. |

`recovery.proof` exposes version, installation UUID, evidence sequence, stored
state, installation identity and optional inventory digest. Activation is bound
to that installation and the order's epoch/Slack `active_since`; CLI show also
prints Team, Enterprise when present, App, Epoch and Active since. A fresh proof
satisfies only the inventory prerequisite and confers no grant authority.
`already_delivered` counts permanent external-delivery suppression;
`external_deliveries` shows reviewed metadata, never fabricated pickup or an
executor attempt. `held_candidates`, `catchup_batches` and `scan` explain held
work. Scan `method`, `code`, `resume_unit` and `coverage_limit` identify incomplete
inspection, including `unknown_older_threads_outside_bounded_history`.
`coverage_origin` records first-window uncertainty. Null metadata means absent,
unknown or unavailable; zero held/delivery counts never prove admission.
`report_statuses` (CLI Report lines) expose fixed-report uncertainty/local-only
outcomes separately from catch-up notices. `retention` lists cleanup jobs,
source_missing/checked_at, and each file/note's recorded status; note `unknown`
is separate from catch-up `notice_unknown`.

Publishing config, including rollback to an earlier config after a failed
reload, can temporarily hold relays while coverage, credentials, policy and
grant authority are reverified. Unrelated publications may do so too. Identity
changes cannot mint new freshness or remove suppression. This does not make
startup-structural connector changes hot-reloadable.

## Legacy rollout without duplicates

An upgraded used or ambiguous order surface stays `legacy_inventory_pending`.
Before enabling catch-up, the coordinator must inventory **all** known manual
receipts and automatic attempts, suppress verified previously delivered
instances, and record explicit out-of-scope dispositions. Never manually relay
old huddles to bypass a hold.

The offline helper source is `scripts/import_huddle_relay_receipts.ml`. Use the
reviewed executable from the remote build, with its hash verified before use:

```text
_build/default/scripts/import_huddle_relay_receipts.exe --db OFFLINE_DB --manifest REVIEWED_JSON
```

Default preview opens the DB **READONLY**, never initializes evidence and never
calls Slack. After reviewed P4 initialization
has stayed closed, stop all daemon DB writers and preserve fresh verified DB
and unit backups. Construct the complete reviewed metadata manifest against
that actual DB's immutable installation UUID, version/bootstrap inventory
scope, current installation identity and real order activation boundaries.
Do not copy a rehearsal UUID, invent activation timestamps or treat one
channel's inventory as the whole installation.

Preview every suppress/link/complete-scope disposition first. Only the reviewed
complete manifest may be applied offline with explicit `--apply`; preserve its
audit receipt, reopen and verify completion/idempotency and exact suppression
before restart/recovery. Delivery suppression does not fabricate an automatic
attempt, nonce, pickup or cap debit. A changed epoch/identity or expired
observation does not erase suppression. This is a coordinator rollout procedure,
not an automatic setup requirement for fresh installations or permission to
mutate a running DB. Whole-branch review, final merged-tree gates, backups,
quiet-turn checks and live rollout remain coordinator work.

## Source cleanup and privacy

Retention checks run independently for active, paused, suspended and cancelled
orders, covering actual relay dispatch/share times in the preceding **30 days**.
Sources are polled at most once per **300 seconds**, subject to backoff. This is
a source-check window, not an automatic deletion deadline. Once cleanup is
outstanding, its verified source/file identities and unfinished work survive
restart and observation expiry until cleanup closes.

A current verified installation and permitted lookup returning `file_not_found`
is deletion evidence. The parser also recognizes an exact-ID file object with
boolean `deleted`, `is_deleted` or `is_tombstoned` true, or `mode: "tombstone"`;
these tombstone forms are synthetic-tested, not a claim about Slack's live
deletion UI. `missing_scope`, `no_permission`, inaccessible/malformed responses,
429, 5xx and transport errors mean **unknown**, with no deletion. Empty or
unavailable transcript content is not deletion evidence. The participant
“Delete AI notes and transcription” behavior remains live-acceptance work;
correctness uses polling, without a `file_deleted` subscription.

Cleanup deletes only the exact Clawq-uploaded file IDs recorded for both retained
attempts. It never deletes source canvases/transcripts, another bot's files or
IDs from editable note text. Imported deliveries need proven Clawq ownership
**and** verified source association; suppression alone does not suffice.
Cleanup remains eligible when the grantor leaves or a target is removed, but
requires current enabled config, all three capabilities, allowed egress and
audit, plus fresh team/app/enterprise/own-user/own-bot credential verification.
A local config/capability refusal makes zero HTTP requests. Mismatch or failed
verification leaves cleanup pending and grants no relay authority.

File deletions and the cleanup-note attempt are recorded before sending.
Restart resumes unfinished deletions; an already-absent own file counts as
removed. An uncertain note is reconciled against one exact own-bot/fire marker
in a complete bounded relay-thread scan, never blindly resent. The fixed note is:

> Clawq removed its relay files because the source was deleted. Clawq cannot recall Anthropic's copy.

Removing Clawq's files cannot retract Anthropic's copy or establish downstream
erasure. CLI pause/cancel sends nothing itself; the daemon may later perform
this verified limited cleanup without resuming the order.

## Egress and rollback

Every standing-order Slack request uses a persisted `Standing_order` snapshot,
an explicit `slack:<channel_id>` access key and current config egress checks.
Relay validation/tracking audit identifies the exact order/fire; resolution
reads have a distinct operation ID per pass. Reports use a fire/reconcile ID
when available, otherwise fixed cause/period identity. Attribution identifies
attempted work, not proven Slack delivery. Never log meeting text, bodies,
tokens or upload capability URLs.

paths below are under `https://slack.com/api/`:

`auth.test` and `canvases.getContent` are reads despite POST. `files.info` uses
the narrow undocumented `include_transcription=true` exception for verified
transcripts. Allocation `files.getUploadURLExternal` is GET. Current Grant uses
`conversations.members`/`users.list`; it does not call `users.conversations`.
Raw binary upload is a separate **POST pinned to `https://files.slack.com/`**, no
redirects and no Authorization header. Egress checks the actual sensitive URI;
logs/audit/errors redact it. Strict egress must admit the actual verbs/paths;
missing audit or policy blocks I/O. Hard authority failures suspend and may
send a fixed management report under current gates. A local capability denial
makes **zero HTTP, including reports**.

Clawq stays silent in retained relay/assistant threads unless explicitly
mentioned; Slackbot never starts turns and history keeps relay data untrusted
with only allowlisted file metadata. Those protections survive restart/pause.
Older binaries can decode `standing_order` as `Room_turn`; do not roll back to
one without reviewing that authority mismatch and the stored data.

P4's remote tests do not complete P5 human/live acceptance. Real room reuse,
separately thread-started huddles, participant deletion and exact Claude notice
wordings still need evidence. Notice matchers remain empty until exact texts
are captured; an unknown notice can count as pickup. SQLite cleanup and
B849–B851 are separate later work, outside this delivery.
